DIVISION 9 - INTERNAL MEMORANDUM 001
DATE: 1994-03-11
FROM: COMMS SUPERVISOR, STATION 7
TO:   ALL DECK OFFICERS

RE: COMMS OUTAGE - DECK 3 AND BELOW

At approximately 0230 hours the primary carrier
link from Relay Station 7 dropped and did not
restore. Backup carrier showed the same failure
pattern. Manual inspection is scheduled for the
0600 shift.

Until further notice, all sublevel communications
will run over the maintenance band. Expect noise
and intermittency. Log every anomaly, however
small, with time and position.

Staff are reminded that the maintenance band is
monitored by this office. Do not use it for
personal traffic.

[END OF MEMORANDUM]
